New Fur Sales Ban

Columbus City Council is considering an ordinance to ban the sale of new fur products. In order for Councilmember Wyche to introduce this ordinance in September, we need to show City Council that this is an important step to protect animals and needs to be a priority.


If you are a Columbus constituent, please click on each councilmember's name below to send them a pre-written email urging their support. Please consider emailing all nine councilmembers; you can do so in just a few clicks. You are encouraged to add your own touch to the message and include your name before sending.
